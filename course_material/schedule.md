## Project schedule

During the six days of project work, there will be one or two times each day where you should be in class: For a status check, where everyone briefly (1-3 minutes) tells about their current status, progress and/or challenges; and for progress presentations where each of you shows on the big screen what you're currently working on (max 10 minutes).

The rest of the time you're free to work from where you like. I will be in class to help out or discuss your projects for about two hours each day. Outside those hours, you can also try to reach me on Discord if you need some assistance.
