### Daily plan

We meet every day at 9:00 in B303-048. 

There will be a lecture/tutorial at first, but most of the day you will be working on your own or in groups. This will be interspersed with ad-hoc sessions on specific issues that come up during your exercises, and with discussions on a relevant topic.

I will not be available in class all day, but I intend to inform you of each day's time schedule (including my availability) the day before on Discord [#announcements](https://discord.com/channels/1527267313563205773/1527267314297470998).
